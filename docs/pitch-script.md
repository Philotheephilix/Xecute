# Xecute — 60 Second Pitch Script

*Slide through as you speak. One slide per section.*

---

**[SLIDE 1 — Hero]**

Everyone scrolls X for alpha. You see $ETH trending, a whale buying, Vitalik posting — that's where trading decisions happen. Not on a CEX. On your feed.

---

**[SLIDE 2 — Problem]**

But the moment you buy, you're naked. ETH drops 20%, you're down 20%. No hedge. No protection. Just loss.

In options markets, traders buy puts to cap their downside. A put gives you the right to sell at a fixed price — a floor. If ETH tanks, you sell at your floor price and walk away whole.

The problem? Options are complex. You need a CEX account, KYC, margin, strike selection, expiry management. Nobody does that from a tweet.

---

**[SLIDE 3 — Solution]**

Xecute packages a put option into a one-click buy. You see $ETH on X, you tap Buy, you pick a floor — say 10% below market — and you're done. Face ID confirms it. Three seconds.

---

**[SLIDE 4 — How it works]**

Under the hood: your USDC swaps to ETH on Ref Finance, a signed floor quote locks your strike price, and the NEAR smart contract holds the guarantee. All in one transaction.

---

**[SLIDE 5 — Why now]**

Two trillion dollar crypto market. Zero products that let you buy with built-in downside protection from social media. We're the first.

---

**[SLIDE 6 — Traction]**

Working MVP on NEAR testnet. Chrome extension live. Floors contract deployed. Full lifecycle — open, sell, close, expire — all verified on-chain.

---

**[SLIDE 7 — Business model]**

We charge a premium on every floor. Like an insurance fee. The riskier the protection, the higher the fee. That's our revenue.

---

**[SLIDE 8 — Tech]**

NEAR Protocol for settlement. WebAuthn passkeys — no seed phrases, just Face ID. Pyth oracle for price feeds. Ed25519 signed quotes. All non-custodial.

---

**[SLIDE 9 — Team]**

Solo builder. Shipped the full stack — contracts, services, extension — in one sprint.

---

**[SLIDE 10 — Ask]**

Xecute. Stop gambling. Start trading with a floor.
