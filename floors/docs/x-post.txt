#NEARHackathon

=== THREAD ===
1/ See a $ETH cashtag in your feed? Our Chrome extension drops a Buy chip right there. Tap it, pick your floor (say 95%), confirm with your fingerprint. One signature. Token lands in your NEAR wallet in seconds.

2/ The floor isn't a stop-loss. It's a smart contract. If the price tanks below your floor, the contract pays the difference. You keep all the upside. Downside is capped. That's the whole idea.

3/ Stack: passkey wallets (WebAuthn), Pyth oracles, Ref Finance swaps, Rust smart contract on NEAR testnet. All open source. Go try it, go break it.
